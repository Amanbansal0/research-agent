# Sarvottam Cables Ltd (SRVCABLE) — Research brief
*Evidence cutoff: 23 September 2026 · Fictional-company test case · Not investment advice*

## Snapshot
Sarvottam Cables manufactures power cables, wires and conductors. In Q1 FY27 (quarter ended 30 June 2026), company-reported revenue was ₹1,248 crore (+18% YoY), EBITDA ₹140 crore (+2.6%), and PAT ₹82 crore (+5.9%); EBITDA margin fell to 11.2% from 12.9%. [S3]
The reported order book was ₹3,900 crore; exports were 21% of revenue. Management said the Bharuch plant was on track for Q3 FY27 commercial production. [S3]

## Bull case
- **Demand and visibility:** order book rose from ₹3,150 crore to ₹3,900 crore year on year; revenue grew 18% in the official release. [S3]
- **Growth options:** management maintained FY27 revenue-growth guidance of 15–17%; data-centre cables are ~9% of domestic revenue, with management expecting that share to double in two years. Bharuch capex is ₹620 crore, ~70% spent. These are management statements, not guarantees. [S2]
- **Share-pledge trend improved:** pledged shares were 4.1% of promoter holding in June 2026, down from 6.0% in March 2026 and 11.5% in June 2025. [S8]

## Bear case
- **Margin pressure / pass-through lag:** EBITDA margin contracted 170 bps; management attributed this mostly to copper and said roughly two-thirds of contracts have price-variation clauses that reset with a 1–2 quarter lag. [S3] [S2]
- **Working capital:** receivable days increased from 78 to 96, driven by two state-utility customers with slower payments; management said it had seen no defaults. [S2]
- **Tax dispute:** a CGST demand order seeks ₹46.3 crore including interest and penalty for FY2020-21/FY2021-22. The company intends to appeal and says it expects no material impact; outcome and cash exposure remain uncertain. [S7]
- **Execution and funding:** Bharuch still needs commissioning and ramp-up; capex is partly funded with term debt and net debt/equity was 0.35x, per management. [S2]

## Open questions
- **Revenue discrepancy:** a secondary article reports ₹1,428 crore and +35% YoY, versus the company IR release's ₹1,248 crore and +18%. This brief privileges the primary company release; verify the exchange-filed results and whether the article contains an error or a different basis. [S1] [S3]
- Can margins recover once copper-cost pass-through catches up, and what is the sensitivity if copper stays elevated? [S2]
- Will the two state-utility customers reduce receivable days, and what is the ageing / concentration of overdue balances? [S2]
- What is the legal timeline, probability-weighted exposure and accounting treatment for the ₹46.3 crore GST demand? [S7]
- An older 2024 article reported promoter pledge at 35%, while the June 2026 exchange summary reports 4.1% of promoter holding pledged. Confirm comparable definitions and whether the earlier episode is fully resolved. [S4] [S8]
